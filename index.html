TECHNOLOGY
ENGINEERED FOR THE NIGHT.

Every hoodie is the Printful All-Over Print Recycled Unisex Hoodie: each panel is printed, then cut and sewn, so the art covers the garment instead of sitting on top of it.

01

Recycled polyester

95% recycled polyester, 5% elastane when made in Latvia. 96% / 4% when made in the US or Mexico.

02

308 g/m² fabric

9.08 oz/yd² fabric weight (may vary by 5%).

03

Soft outside, fleece inside

A soft, cotton-feel fabric face with brushed fleece on the inside.

04

Double-lined hood

A double-lined hood that comes with drawstrings.

05

Cut & sew print

All-over synthetic print: panels are printed edge to edge, then cut and sewn with overlock seams.

06

Unisex style

Unisex sizing. Check the size chart on each Etsy listing before you order.

05 — FAQ

NEED TO
KNOW.

Still unsure? Message us through our Etsy shop and we will help.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is free on every hoodie, so no shipping fee is added at Etsy checkout.

How long until my hoodie arrives?

Each hoodie is printed on demand by Printful after you order, so it takes some days to produce before it ships. Etsy shows the estimated delivery date on the listing and at checkout.

Which size should I pick?

Please check the size chart on the Etsy listing of your hoodie before ordering and compare it with a hoodie you already own. The cut is unisex.

Can I return or exchange it?

Returns and exchanges follow our shop policy on Etsy, which you can read on the listing page and in the shop's policies section. Because every piece is made to order, please check sizing carefully first.

How do I use the discount code?

Enter in the coupon field at Etsy checkout to get 10% off.

Where do I pay?

All orders are placed and paid on Etsy. Every “Buy on Etsy” button opens the matching listing in a new tab.
